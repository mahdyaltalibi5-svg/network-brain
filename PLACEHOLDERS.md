# PLACEHOLDERS — facts the client must supply

This list will be regenerated from the code (`npm run placeholders`) once Phase 1 begins. Expected items so far:

- Street address, suite, ZIP · phone · email · geo coordinates
- Opening hours (incl. by-appointment policy)
- Founder name, photo, training/credentials, years of experience
- Prices: bespoke two-piece / three-piece starting price, made-to-measure starting price, image consulting packages, wedding party pricing → `priceRange`
- Fabric mills and cloth books carried
- Production timelines and number of fittings (confirm studio-specific numbers)
- Real client reviews/testimonials (enables AggregateRating schema)
- Social profiles for `sameAs` (Instagram, Google Business Profile, etc.)
- Booking provider URL
- Logo files (SVG) and photography
