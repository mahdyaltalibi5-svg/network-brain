Suit or Tuxedo

Dressed for
the setting.

For a formal evening reception, a bespoke tuxedo in black or midnight navy, with satin or grosgrain peak or shawl lapels. A garment you own for every black-tie event that follows.

For daytime, outdoor and mountain weddings from Salt Lake City to Park City, a three-piece suit is a favourite. The waistcoat keeps the look sharp when the jacket comes off for the dancing.

Wedding Questions

Before the
big day.

How far ahead should I order a custom wedding suit?

Start 4 to 6 months before the wedding. The suit typically takes 5 to 8 weeks from consultation to final fitting, and the extra time leaves room for decisions and a final fitting close to the date.

Can you dress the groomsmen and family too?

Yes. We dress grooms, groomsmen, fathers and family. Have everyone measured at least three months before the date so all the pieces are made together.

Should the groom wear a suit or a tuxedo?

For a formal evening reception, a tuxedo. For daytime, outdoor and mountain weddings, a three-piece suit is a favourite; the waistcoat keeps the look sharp when the jacket comes off.

Do you offer rush wedding suits?

Rush orders are available in select circumstances. Call (801) 866-9718 as early as you can.

Can the consultation be done by video?

Yes. The first consultation can be in person in Salt Lake City or by video, which helps when the wedding party is spread out.
