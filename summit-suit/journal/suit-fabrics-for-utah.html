The Journal

Choosing cloth
for Utah.

Salt Lake City has hot, dry summers, cold winters, and days that change by thirty degrees between the morning meeting and the evening dinner. Your cloth should be chosen for that.

Book a Free ConsultationAll articles

By Summit Customs, Salt Lake City · October 23, 2026 · 3 min read

Start with a year-round worsted

If you are buying one suit, make it a mid-weight worsted wool. Wool breathes, resists wrinkles, and handles a dry climate well. A Super 110s to 130s cloth is the sweet spot for most men: refined enough for a boardroom, durable enough for weekly wear. Navy and charcoal work in every season.

For summer: open weaves and linen blends

Utah summers are hot, but dry, so you need breathability more than moisture management. Look for lighter weights and open weaves. Wool-linen blends give the cool feel of linen with fewer wrinkles. Our 2026 Spring/Summer catalog includes an Angelico linen and wool range and an Olimpias summer corduroy, alongside lightweight Marlane cloths.

For winter: weight and texture

For winter, choose heavier cloths: flannel, tweed and textured wools that hold warmth and look right with an overcoat. A sport coat in a bold winter texture is often a better buy than a second winter suit, because it works with trousers and denim too.

For travel and long days: stretch and performance

If you fly often, or wear your suit from morning to night, a cloth with natural stretch or a high-twist yarn recovers from creasing and moves with you. The Marlane Extender® 4 Seasons and Vivo Stretch ranges in our catalog are built for exactly this.

Colour and the mountain light

Utah's high-altitude sun is bright and clear. Mid-blues, greys and earth tones look rich in it; very pale cloths can look washed out in photographs taken outdoors at midday. For evening, midnight navy reads deeper than black under artificial light.

Understanding Super numbers

The Super number measures fibre fineness, not quality on its own. Higher numbers (150s to 180s) feel softer and drape beautifully but are more delicate. Lower numbers (100s to 120s) are more durable. You don't need to know the difference before you arrive; that is Kobe's job. You just need to know how you want to feel when you wear it.

Quick Answers

In short.

What is the best suit fabric for summer in Salt Lake City?

A lightweight, open-weave wool or a wool-linen blend. Both breathe well in dry heat, and the wool content keeps wrinkles under control.

What weight of suit should I buy first?

A mid-weight worsted wool, around Super 110s to 130s, in navy or charcoal. It works across most of the year in Utah.

Can I see the fabrics before my consultation?

Yes. The current seasonal catalog is online at summitcustomsuits.com/fabrics, and the full library of 4,000+ cloths is available in person.
