The Journal

Black tie in
Salt Lake City.

Galas, charity dinners, formal weddings. If black tie shows up on your calendar more than once a year, here is what the dress code really asks for, and why owning beats renting.

Book a Free ConsultationAll articles

By Summit Customs, Salt Lake City · October 23, 2026 · 3 min read

What black tie requires

  • A dinner jacket (tuxedo jacket) in black or midnight navy, with a peak lapel or shawl collar faced in satin or grosgrain. Never a notch lapel.
  • Matching trousers with a braid down the outside seam, worn with side adjusters or braces rather than a belt.
  • A white dress shirt with a turn-down collar, and a black bow tie.
  • Black shoes: patent or highly polished calf Oxfords. A black cap-toe Oxford is the classic choice.

A tuxedo is not a black suit

A tuxedo is a different garment with its own rules of proportion, fabric and finish. The silk facings catch the light; the cloth, usually barathea wool, absorbs it. A black business suit with a bow tie reads as exactly that.

Black or midnight navy?

Black tie does not mandate black cloth. Midnight navy often appears blacker than black under artificial light, and it is a historic tradition that commands presence. It is the colour Kobe wears himself.

Rent or own?

A rental is cut for someone else and fitted with clips and compromises. If you attend black tie once in your life, rent. If it shows up every year (a gala, a firm dinner, a wedding season), a tuxedo cut to your measurements quickly costs less per wear than repeated rentals, and you never again wonder how it will fit. Your pattern stays on file, so if your build changes, a new one is simple.

Black tie optional

"Black tie optional" means a tuxedo is welcome, and a dark suit in navy or charcoal with a tie is acceptable. Owning a tuxedo makes the decision for you.

Quick Answers

In short.

Can I wear a black suit to a black tie event?

It is not correct black tie. A tuxedo has silk-faced lapels and braided trousers; a black suit does not. At a "black tie optional" event a dark suit is acceptable.

Is midnight navy acceptable for black tie?

Yes. Midnight navy is a traditional black-tie colour and often looks deeper than black under artificial light.

Should I rent or buy a tuxedo?

If you attend black-tie events regularly, owning a tuxedo cut to your measurements costs less per wear than repeated rentals and always fits.
