The garments.

How It Works

The process.

  1. 01

    The Consultation

    In person in Salt Lake City or by video. We begin with a conversation about your lifestyle, occasions, and the impression you want to leave. Nothing is measured until we understand the man being dressed.

  2. 02

    The Cloth

    You select from an extensive library of wools, linens, tweeds, and tropicals from Dormeuil, Scabal, Vitale Barberis Canonico, and Holland & Sherry. We guide you toward what works for your body, climate, and occasion.

  3. 03

    The Construction

    Your pattern is drawn to your body, not adapted from a standard block. Every seam, lining, and detail is considered. The garment is built around you, not bent to fit you.

  4. 04

    The Fitting

    You try on something built entirely for you, with final refinements made on the spot. Your pattern stays on file, so reorders take half the time and need only one fitting.
