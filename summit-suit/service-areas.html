Downtown, our home

Salt Lake City

Our atelier is at 215 S State St, Suite 575, in the heart of downtown, a short walk from the City Creek, Main Street and the law and finance offices along State Street. Consultations are by appointment, Monday to Saturday.

About 35 minutes via I-80

Park City

Park City clients come to us for mountain weddings, winter events and tailoring that works between the slopes and the boardroom. Ask about lighter cloths for summer ceremonies and textured winter jackets.

About 30 minutes via I-15

Lehi & Silicon Slopes

For founders and executives along Silicon Slopes, we build suits and sport coats for investor meetings, conferences and the dinners that follow. Video consultations make the first step easy around a busy schedule.

About 45 minutes via I-15

Provo & Orem

From graduation and first-job suits to wedding parties, Utah County clients make the drive for a first consultation, and use video for follow-up conversations.

About 20 to 25 minutes

Sandy, Draper & South Jordan

South valley professionals, from the Draper tech corridor to Sandy, can reach the downtown atelier quickly on I-15 or TRAX.

About 40 minutes via I-15

Ogden & Layton

Northern Utah clients book for weddings, black-tie events and business wardrobes. Plan a single in-person consultation and handle details by video.

About 15 minutes

Bountiful & Davis County

Just north of downtown, Bountiful and Davis County clients are minutes away from fittings.
