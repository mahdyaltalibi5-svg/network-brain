Questions

Before you
begin.

What is image consulting?

Image consulting is a personal strategy for how you present yourself: what you wear, how it fits, and what it communicates in the rooms that matter to you. At Summit Customs it is built around your work, your occasions and your goals.

Do I have to buy clothes to work with you?

No. The goal is to understand what you need, not to sell you the most. Many clients start with a conversation about what they already own.

Can image consulting be done by video?

The first consultation can be held in person in Salt Lake City or by video.
