Tier 03

The Standard

For the man who already knows exactly who he is.

This is the top of what we do. Reserved for clients who come in with a clear vision of what they want, and the patience to let the craft meet it.

No compromises. The finest available fabrics, full canvas construction, and a level of detail work that takes longer because it should. Built once. Worn for decades.

How It Works

From conversation
to yours.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    30 minutes, in person or by video. We talk about where you're going, what you need the suit to do, and which tier fits the occasion and your style.

  2. 02

    Selection

    Fabric, colour, and detail choices. You leave knowing exactly what you ordered and why. No guesswork.

  3. 03

    Fittings

    Your measurements guide the garment. Fittings refine its balance and details before delivery.

  4. 04

    Yours

    Delivered. Fitted. If anything needs adjusting, we handle it. You leave with a suit built for one person: you.
