Overview

Summit Customs ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you use our website or book a consultation.

Information we collect

When you book a consultation or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Preferred consultation date and time
  • Any notes or details you share in your message

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Schedule and confirm your consultation appointment
  • Send you booking confirmations and reminders via email
  • Follow up regarding your consultation or services
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate our booking and communication systems:

  • Google Calendar, used to schedule and manage consultation appointments. Your booking details are added to a private business calendar.
  • EmailJS, used to send booking confirmation emails. Your name and email are transmitted securely to deliver these notifications.

Data retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, primarily to manage your consultation and any ongoing service relationship. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

We do not sell your data

Summit Customs does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is used exclusively to serve you as a client.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of any future communications at any time

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out: kobe@summitcustomsuits.com · (801) 866-9718
