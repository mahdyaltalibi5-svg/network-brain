Conceived Independently

Not a suit jacket
missing its trousers.

A true sport coat is conceived independently. The cut is softer, the texture is richer, and the details speak to its distinct purpose. It pairs as naturally with tailored denim as it does with worsted wool trousers.

We build blazers and sport coats around the way you plan to wear them. Whether it is an unstructured linen piece for summer evenings or a heavier cloth for winter layers, the fitting begins with you.
